## Hi, I'm Shane 👋

I'm a developer and data scientist. I build small, focused apps, often ones that pair cheap hardware with a phone to measure things that used to need a lab.

I studied Operations Research at **Cornell**, where I also pole vaulted, and I competed for **Kentucky** after that. Most of my side projects come out of sports.

### What I'm building

- **[Way Speed](https://wayspeed.net)** *(beta)*: one LiDAR sensor beside the track records a full sprint velocity curve and automatic splits after every rep. Built with ROS 2, Python and a React Native companion app.
- **[Photo Painter](https://github.com/srace11/ESP32-S3-PhotoPainter)** *(coming soon)*: custom firmware for an ESP32-S3 e-paper frame, plus a mobile app that turns your photos into paintings and sends them to the frame.
- **Volleyball Jump Mechanics** *(coming soon)*: analyzes your approach and jump so you can add height at the net.

More at **[shaneracey.com](https://shaneracey.com)**.
